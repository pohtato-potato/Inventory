# Inventory

Written 15 November 2026 from the owner's answers (rounds 14–15) and the spec at `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-inventory-design.md`. The spec is the authority; this file is the short product context for design work.

## What it is
A phone app holding everything one person owns: clothes and everything else, equally. A reference app: opened when there is a question (what do I own, where is it, what goes with what, do I already have one, what should I buy), not every day.

## Who uses it
One owner, on a Samsung S23, installed as a web app. Checked at 360 × 745 and 340 × 700. Usually standing at a cupboard, in a shop, or adding pieces in a bulk afternoon with clothes laid flat.

## Platform
web (installed PWA, phone only, offline).

## Brand commitments
- Dark app. Things sit on graphite; an opened thing floods the screen with its colour. Big confident type.
- Chalk primary buttons; colour comes from the owner's things and their kinds. No decorative colour.
- Prices of owned things are hidden while browsing; shown on the item page and Numbers only.
- Part of a family with Health: may look different, but takes Health's good traits (motion, confident numbers, radiance that means something).
- Motion is wanted. No reduced-motion or battery-saver mode in the app.
- No religious or astrological motifs. No cute mascots. No corporate-minimal blandness.
- Legibility baseline kept quietly: 16 px body, nothing under 14 px, 4.5:1 contrast, no thin or handwriting fonts, 44 px targets.

## Voice
Plain words, short sentences, no jargon. "Pieces", "places", "wishes".
