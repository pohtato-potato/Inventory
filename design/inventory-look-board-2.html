Tried and set aside

Four more ideas came up and lost. One thing from each is worth keeping:

  • A fan-convention catalogue (hundreds of tiny thumbnails on newsprint). Lost because one-ink paper throws away your clothes' colour. Kept: the option of a denser grid, four or five across, so you see more of the wardrobe at once.
  • An old airline ticket wallet. Lost because it is a light, paper look and the app stays dark. Kept: a piece you let go is stamped "gone" and stays in place, it never just disappears.
  • A printed design annual (white sheet, crosshair marks). Lost for the same reason: light. Kept: photos are never tinted or filtered.
  • A reference book page with a centre rail of keys. Lost because it is built for reading text, and this app is pictures. Nothing kept.
