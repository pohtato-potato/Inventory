Inventory redesign, round 1: breadth

Seven different worlds for the same app, each shown on two live phones. Every one keeps the app's parts: the piece count, a featured piece, browse by kind, colour and place, counts on every filter, fit at a glance, logging what you wore, wishes, numbers, and the add button in the middle of the tab bar.

  • All of them add real colour. Colour always has a job (a piece's own colour, a place, a kind, or a state), so none of it is decoration.
  • The phones animate on a loop. Press Replay under a phone to watch from the start.
  • React to each world with Love, Like, Unsure or No, and say which parts you would steal from it. Mixing is expected in round 2.
  • The clothes are example photos. Names, counts and prices are made up.
