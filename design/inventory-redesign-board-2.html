Inventory redesign, round 2

What I heard

  • It holds everything you own: laptop, power bank, headphones, tools and home things, not only clothes. Clothing metaphors (rails, laundry tags, a dressing room) are out.
  • Theatre and marquee lights were far too flashy.
  • The night-street Numbers was impossible to read.

What I kept

  • Shade Card's piece page: the whole screen takes the item's colour, flooding out from where you tapped. It's now the signature move for every item, clothes and things alike.
  • The "Do I own this?" camera answer that rises as a card.
  • Plain, general app layout. Colour comes from your things and does the talking.

Connecting…

One look, shared by all three

Colour, and the job each does

Graphite. The ground. Neutral, so every item's colour reads true.
Each item's own colour. Fills its tile, and floods its whole page when opened.
A colour per kind of thing (Electronics blue, Home violet, Tools green…). Used only when an item has no photo or colour of its own yet, so a laptop with no photo still gets a page with character.
Chalk. The main button on every screen, because it reads on any item colour.
Green and amber. Fit only, as before.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for names and numbers (the face from the screen you liked). Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything you read.

Motion

Calm and useful. Tiles rise into place, the tapped item floods the screen, numbers count up, bars grow, answers slide up from the bottom. Nothing blinks or performs.
