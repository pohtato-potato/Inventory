Cut-out test

Throwaway test for Inventory. Can the S23 cut a piece of clothing out of a photo, in the browser, with nothing uploaded, and still do it in airplane mode?

  1. Online: pick an engine, take a photo of a shirt. The first run downloads the model (44 MB on WASM).
  2. Try a few: on a bed, on a hanger, on the floor, worn.
  3. Turn on airplane mode, close this tab, reopen the page, run one more.
  4. Screenshot the results table and send it to Claude.
