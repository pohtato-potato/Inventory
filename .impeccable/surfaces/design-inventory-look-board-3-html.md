# Inventory app screens (Operate)

Scope: every Build 1 screen of the Inventory phone app, plus the Build 2 outfits preview. Audience: the owner, at a cupboard or in a shop, asking what they own and where it is. Constraints: S23 at 360 × 745 and 340 × 700; dark; no thin type; prices hidden while browsing. Unresolved: places shape, grid density.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Shop Window. The owner's own pieces are the display; the app is the dark street around a lit window. It refuses the flat grey catalogue grid with one accent.

OWN-WORLD: near-black ground; every piece stands in a "window", a dark panel lit from behind by a soft pool of that piece's own colour, with a darker floor. Condensed medium-weight numerals, widely spaced caps for labels, a plain legible sans for everything else. One blue for the main action. Green, amber and blue-grey only for fit.

STORY: the owner opens the app, sees one piece in the window with a reason, sees how many pieces they own, and reaches any piece by kind, colour or place in two taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a window across the top half: the count as a large numeral top left, one large piece lit bottom right, its name and reason bottom left. Below: by kind / by colour / by place switch, kind chips with counts, three-across grid of small windows. Tab bar with the blue add button centred.

FORM: Shop Window, candidate 1 of 7 on the grounded list, chosen by the owner over the assigned lead. seed:93619e43564552f9. Signature move: the window light takes each piece's colour, and lights up when a screen opens.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
